# Chroma v3.3: Razan versus Briga combat and XP repair

Player pointed out that the first Black-Shadow Razan encounter awarded 55 XP
while a Briga awarded 65 XP, despite the campaign treating Razan as a
more substantial ground threat. Both monsters are **D-rank** in Solo Leveling,
appear jointly at Hapjeong B2, and canon does not establish a precise
power or XP ranking. The numerical hierarchy below is a crossover design
decision, not a claim that the original author assigned specific stats.

## Level-7 campaign rebalance

- Black-Shadow Razan: System threat 10 (was 8); base XP 72 (was 52),
  so effective single-target XP **83** at Player Level 7 (+15% threat).
  More robust ground panther: strength24/stamina23/agility25/
  perception22; HP210 armor4 weaponForce9.
- Razor-Clawed Briga: remains threat9/baseXP59, effective XP65 at Lv7;
  retains superior top-down attack mobility and deadly claws. It is not
  a free victory simply because Razan now has higher threat rating.
- The existing Razan victory ref chroma.instant.razan.01 occurred at
  D3 17:46 (sourceRef chroma.d3_b2_razan_quicksilver_combo_6.554)
  and paid 55 XP. The existing Briga victory ref chroma.instant.briga.01
  (sourceRef chroma.d3_b2_briga_close_combo_7.563) paid 65 XP.
- ONE evidence-locked repair action, exactly
  chroma.repair.d3_razan_reward_28xp.584, can add **+28 XP**
  to the already-earned Razan reward (83-55), ONLY in the real Chroma
  campaign, at D3 18:24, with both authenticated defeatEvidence,
  historical ledger events and exact Level7 XP1459.
  No new kill or System gold, no HP/MP adjustments, no double award,
  and the action cannot be replayed. End XP expected1487/1853.
- No retroactive changes to Briga rewards, previous HP damage,
  mastery, inventory, title, or room position.
