## New NPC initiative mechanic (staging implementation)

**Independence ≠ reaction-only.** In a verified same-scene meeting Vesha may voluntarily start a conversation, comment on her own day, state curiosity or warmth, ask about a matter she actually knows, choose to set limits or decide to return to customers—without a fresh user question. She can also simply be busy, not speak, or stop talking. Having a social impulse is not itself an automatic committed change or proof of love.

- `planNpcInitiativeV4` creates only **possible**, source-anchored initiative types: `SPEAK_FIRST`, `EXPRESS_PERSONAL_STANCE`, `SET_BOUNDARY`, `ACT_ON_OWN_GOAL`, `PRIORITIZE_OWN_DUTY`, and `OUTREACH` through a verified channel. It never generates dialogue, feelings-as-fact, movement, money or messages. GM/V3 makes the causal individual decision and only semantic commit writes it.
- The plan cites live actor goal, directed relationship evidence and past memory origins. An internal feeling may develop from own events, values and current experience, not solely from Shura's dialogue. GM must resolve concrete emotion and expression from evidence; the planner never fabricates a past emotion or assumes user reciprocity.
- Co-presence in code is gated by committed `CONTROL`/`ACTIVE_CONTEXT` matching with specific `participating_npc` IDs; a matching *district* alone is insufficient. Final speech still needs actual conversational proximity, time and canonical Taren comprehension.
- Exact physical consent is **per act**; existing consent to one hug or walk is not a lifetime grant. An NPC can initiate a polite verbal request or compliment without pre-authorized player response, but cannot auto-choose Shura's reply, movement or contact consent.
- In the absence of a usable channel and a verified discretionary window, an offscreen NPC may continue her own life but must not send a fictitious message or teleport to Shura. Separate valid `npc_decision.lifeV4` and real commute is required if physical contact results.
- Routine 30-minute review eligibility does **not** imply 30-minute emotional swings or automatic attention to Shura. NPC-initiated contact should not be a mandatory repeated event; source IDs from previous initiatives can suppress repetition.
