# Daedalus: seven-day Living World behavioral audit

**Alternative campaign branch, NOT a rewrite of any previous session.** Player never retracts application to Hephaestus. He joins Loki on the same first day. By day seven he has demonstrated M1, assembled communications apparatus privately and built/demonstrated an AWP-pattern long rifle. The existing prior campaign application was a separate branch; this scenario deliberately keeps the application open.

## Identity and first contact

Daedalus is 16, of a rare ancient race, and appears androgynous: long dark hair with blue tips, clock-hand bronze-gold eyes, round spectacles, glowing skin patterns, hovering halo. Sex-changing is an ability, **not common NPC knowledge until demonstrated**. New citizens and NPCs don't inherit others' familiarity. The name coincides with the famous Orario architect Daedalus; the city even has Daedalus Street. A clerk, a craftswoman or a god familiar with history can ask if it is a nickname or lineage, but **must not infer kinship with the Knossos architect or secret labyrinth knowledge automatically**.

## In-character reaction suggestions (not scripted compulsory dialogue)

**Guild/city:** A halo and moving hand-shaped eye mechanisms get conspicuous glances; the name "Daedalus" may prompt a question. Nobody magically knows the family's historical secrets, and nobody shrugs off the appearance as instantly ordinary.

**Tsubaki:** Appears energetic, confident and practical, tests the candidate directly, jokes about a famous name, asks what the strange ability can do in a forge. After discovering his Loki blessing she is annoyed at losing a candidate and wants a proper answer; she may still seek a collaborative craft test. She does not know the private AWP results.

**Hephaestus:** Professional rather than passively indifferent. On day two she hears an unusual apprentice succeeded at trial, so she requests an interview or further technical detail. On day four she is informed Loki has already given Falna and considers a decision; she may refuse recruitment while retaining non-Familia collaboration. Her goddess status is not telepathy.

**Loki:** Makes sharp jokes about the halo, moving-eye clockwork and the celebrated name; cautious protectiveness and selfish familia pride can coexist. After witnessing M1, novelty turns into a strategic question. Loki does *not* know later AWP details before a legitimate report.

**Finn:** Screens new recruit for risk and loyalty; his direct knowledge of Loki Falna is grounded. He receives Raul's M1 report, later Tiona's rifle report and inspects the iron plate. He evaluates real tactical implications without equating missing velocity numbers with missing observed penetration. Restricts uncontrolled disclosure and asks for further tests, without claiming guaranteed threat to Ottar.

**Riveria:** May regard exotic appearance with discipline and caution, but if she has not seen the guns or communications prototype she has no detailed commentary on their mechanisms. More interest once real evidence arrives through a proper relay.

**Tiona:** Impulsive curiosity about Daedalus and both gun demonstrations; might eagerly ask for a trial shot or joke about what can be hit from the ramparts. Does not instantly understand metallurgy or terminal ballistics.

**Tione:** More defensive about reckless testing and Finn's safety, can quarrel with Tiona. Genuinely alarmed by physical perforation without automatic deference to inventor.

**Aiz:** Quietly watches and compares weapon to movement and sword timing, not a generic praising crowd member. M1 witness but no proof of AWP unless later told.

**Lefiya:** Interested, startled and potentially protective of magical expertise, but curiosity need not become jealousy every time. M1 witness, communications prototype private.

**Bete:** Blunt mockery of the strange newcomer; he is not a flustered nice boy. In this specific witness map he has *not* seen M1 or AWP, therefore cannot acknowledge or dismiss their proven results until a real account arrives. No supernatural information transfer.

**Raul & instructor:** Witnessed M1 and can report method and demonstrated results; Raul briefs Finn. Instructor also saw AWP test and enforces realistic shooting-range limits. Their reports are not automatically public rumors.

## Interpretation test

The world is not realistic if every witness says "wow" or every absentee denies a real test. Contrasting, durable reactions are required. Hephaestus reviewing an application is distinct from knowing the Loki Familia's restricted technology. The owner of a pending application is obliged to progress or reject it; a week of unexplained silence would be a failure.
